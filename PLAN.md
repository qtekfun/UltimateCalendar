## Phase 2 — Logic
- [x] **T07 Invitation detector**: "me" per calendar + aliases; pending = own attendee `NEEDS-ACTION` on future events of any calendar; differences between runs (new, changed, cancelled, answered elsewhere). **100 % coverage.**
  *Result:* `InvitationDetector(clock)` in `domain/invitations`: `scan` (pending + state of each event) and `diff` (new, changed, cancelled, answered elsewhere); 100 % lines and branches.
- [x] **T08 Periodic check**: WorkManager with the interval from Settings, `requestSync` (per T02), run on open/refresh/`ContentObserver`; record of what has already been notified in Room.
  *Result:* `InvitationChecker` (`sync/`) requests the sync, scans all calendars with `InvitationDetector`, notifies the difference to `InvitationNotifier` and only then replaces, in a transaction, the Room table `notified_invitations` (v2 with migration 1→2 and test): if the check dies halfway nothing is saved and the next one notifies what is pending. `InvitationCheckCoordinator` offers `checkNow()`, `onAppOpened()` and observes `CalendarSource.changes` with debounce; the periodic work (WorkManager on demand, with its own factory and no new dependencies) comes from `InvitationCheckSettings` (15 min by default, T23 binds it) and the notifier is a no-op until T21. **`requestSync` on real accounts (Google, DAVx5) is not verified until T02**: it is best-effort and tolerates failure. `CalendarSource` still has no Hilt binding until T05: it is resolved with `@BindsOptionalOf`, with `UnavailableCalendarSource` in the meantime.
- [x] **T09 Recurrence logic**: edit/delete "only this / this and following / all" (`RRULE` cut with `UNTIL`, exceptions, new series). Presets and custom copied from the UltimateTasks editor. **100 % coverage in the split.**
  *Result:* `RecurrenceRule.until` becomes `Until` (`Day` for all-day, UTC `Moment` for timed events); `RecurrenceSplitter` returns `SeriesChange` (without expanding recurrences: the caller supplies `occurrencesBefore` for `COUNT`); presets and `CustomRepeat` copied from UltimateTasks.
- [x] **T10 Event reminder scheduler**: copy and adapt `ReminderPlanner` (per-instance reminders, all-day, snooze), recovery of missed ones, heartbeat and robust mode. **100 % coverage** in scheduler and recovery.
  - *Result:* snooze (5 min / 15 min / 1 h) and dismiss from the notification, with "Join" (Meet, Zoom, Teams, Jitsi, Whereby links in the location or description) or "Map" (`geo:`) depending on the event; "Snooze" swaps the buttons for the three durations (Android shows three at most). What is snoozed is saved in SharedPreferences, enters the alarm plan (survives a reboot) and is reconciled with recovery (atomic `take`: it never fires twice); if the event is edited or cancelled it stops ringing. Three channels in `NotificationChannels` (reminders, invitations, changes). Pure logic in `domain/reminders` at 100 %. Not tested on a real phone. The real event source was wired in #63 (`data.reminders.CalendarReminderEventSource` on top of `CalendarSource.instancesWithReminders`, bulk read); until then the Hilt module used an empty source and no reminder was scheduled.
- [x] **T11 Reliability tests**: app killed halfway through a check, time zone change, reboot, update, daylight saving time.
  - *Result:* JVM suite in `app/src/test/.../reliability` on the real classes (coordinator, recovery, heartbeat, invitation checker, detector, in-memory Room, `FakeCalendarSource`) with fakes only at the Android edges (alarms, notifications). It covers: invitation check killed at every read and at the notification (the next one notifies everything, once); time zone and time change (TIME_SET/TIMEZONE_CHANGED); reboot and update, also with snoozed reminders; daylight saving time in Madrid, New York, Kolkata and Lord Howe (days of 23/23.5/24.5/25 h, reminders inside the gap and the overlap, daily series); recovery window 6/24/48 h/never, no avalanche the first time, snoozed never twice; events edited, moved and cancelled after scheduling or reminding. It found a real bug (repeated all-day reminders on time zone change), fixed. `BootActions` is checked against the manifest. It still needs the author's phone (ColorOS): that the system really delivers the alarms after reboot/update/time change, the behavior with the app frozen or killed by the manufacturer, and the real notifications.
